# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND TRADE SECRET PROTECTION AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **Odynex Axis Logistics LLC**, a Texas Limited Liability Company ("Disclosing Party"), and the accessing or evaluating legal enterprise entity ("Receiving Party").

### 1. Recitals & Trade Secret Intentions
The Parties intend to engage in a technical evaluation of the ScreeTC Real-Time Physics Telemetry Infrastructure layer. In connection with this evaluation, Disclosing Party will provide Receiving Party access to proprietary source code frameworks, Nvidia container orchestrations, and trained Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) core architectures ("Proprietary Assets").

### 2. Statutory Governance (TUTSA)
Receiving Party explicitly acknowledges that the Proprietary Assets provided constitute highly valuable, independent economic trade secrets under the **Texas Uniform Trade Secrets Act (TUTSA) (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code Chapter 134A)**. 
* Receiving Party shall enforce maximum reasonable security measures to protect these assets from public disclosure or data extraction.
* The source code tracking architectures, tensor parameter dimensions, and anonymization pipelines must not be reverse-engineered, decompiled, or committed to any public code repository index or public AI model training scraper pipeline.

### 3. Term and Defensibility Moats
This Agreement and the obligations of confidentiality maintained under TUTSA shall survive for a period of five (5) years from the date of repository access execution, or for as long as the disclosed Proprietary Assets retain independent economic value as unpublicized trade secrets, whichever duration is longer. Any breach or misappropriation of these parameters yields immediate grounds for injunctive relief under Texas civil procedure guidelines.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, access to the code infrastructure repository constitutes acceptance of the programmatic constraints detailed herein by both duly authorized corporate representatives.
